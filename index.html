Speed: what to expect

Clear picture

The app shows your website, so pages load as fast as tucokids.com loads on a phone today. If the website is fast, the app is fast. If a page is slow on the website, it is slow in the app too.

What helps: the tab bar and opening screen are part of the app itself, so taps feel instant even while a page loads. Pages a parent has already visited open faster the second time. Any speed work done on the website improves the app automatically.

What we can add: your site runs many scripts (small programs your apps and trackers load on every page). With your approval, we can switch off trackers that aren't needed for app visitors, such as Contentsquare or VWO, so app pages load lighter.

Your third-party apps in the app

We checked every app that customers see on tucokids.com, and all 23 work inside the app. 20 of them, including Judge.me, Nector, Whatmore, Searchanise, KwikPass and ClickPost, need nothing at all. The 3 below need a small fix that we build in before launch.

GoKwik checkoutOne-click checkout, COD, UPI

When a parent pays by UPI, the app must hand over to GPay or PhonePe and bring them back. We build this handover into the app and test it with real ₹1 orders before launch.

ShopfloFast checkout

Same UPI handover as GoKwik. Built and tested the same way.

CleverTapNotifications and journeys

Website notifications don't reach an app. We add CleverTap's app kit, so your team sends app notifications from the same CleverTap dashboard.

Not affected: admin-only tools (such as Metafield Updates) and your catalogue channels (Facebook & Instagram, Google & YouTube, Pinterest) keep working as they do now. Customers never see them.
If an app ever doesn't behave inside the app

1. We catch it before launch. Every app on this list is tested inside the app on iPhone and Android.

2. A safety net. Any single page that misbehaves can open in a browser panel that slides up inside the app, where it works exactly like the website.

3. We fix it at the source. We work with that app's team to fix it, with an introduction from you where needed.

4. Your website is never touched. Nothing we do in the app can affect tucokids.com.

Process

01

Plan and set up accounts

Agree the tab bar, app name, icon and store screenshots, and which notifications to send. Your team opens Google Play and Apple developer accounts in Tuco's name, with our help.

02

Build the shell

The app frame around tucokids.com: tab bar, opening and offline screens, CleverTap notifications, smart links, the UPI handover and app-order tracking.

03

Test everything

Real ₹1 orders on UPI, card and COD. Login, rewards, reviews, videos, quiz and every app on the list. Your team tries it on their own phones through test versions of the app before anyone else sees it.

04

Submit to the stores

Store listings, privacy policy and the stores' data forms. We handle Google and Apple review end to end.

05

Launch

"Get the app" banner on the website, a WhatsApp and CleverTap announcement, a QR code on pack inserts, and an optional app-only first-order offer.

06

Measure

A regular readout of installs, the app's share of orders, repeat purchase rate and notification opens.

What we need from Tuco
  1. Developer accounts: Google Play and Apple developer accounts in Tuco's name, and a D-U-N-S number for Apple if you don't have one.
  2. Shopify access: staff access to Checkout and Payments settings, so we can confirm the GoKwik setup.
  3. Approvals: app name, icon and store listing, and which trackers can be switched off for app visitors.
  4. Introductions: to app vendors, only if one needs a fix on their side.
  5. Launch support: a slot in your WhatsApp and CleverTap calendar, and a decision on an app-only offer.
