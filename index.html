We will turn tucokids.com into an app that customers can download from the Play Store and App Store. The app shows your website exactly as it is: the same products, quiz, reviews, rewards, videos and GoKwik checkout. Nothing changes in your Shopify setup, and your team keeps working the same way.

Scope

The app is an exact replica of tucokids.com. It has the same pages, sections, products, prices, offers, apps, checkout and login. We do not redesign or rebuild anything. The only new part is the shell around the website: the bottom tab bar, notifications, the opening and offline screens, and smart links.

What we're building

A wrapper app is an app that loads your live website inside it. Around the website is the shell, which is the part we build: the app icon, the bottom buttons and notifications. When you update your website, the app updates too.

Your third-party apps

We checked every app your customers see on tucokids.com. All 23 work in the app. 20 of them, including Judge.me, Nector, Whatmore, Searchanise, KwikPass and ClickPost, need no changes. The 3 below need a small fix, which we do before launch.

Need a small fix from us

3 apps · fixed before launch
GoKwik checkoutOne-click checkout, COD, UPI

When a customer pays by UPI, the app has to open GPay or PhonePe and then come back to the app. We build this in and test it with real ₹1 orders before launch.

ShopfloFast checkout

Same UPI fix as GoKwik. Built and tested the same way.

CleverTapNotifications and journeys

Website notifications do not work in an app. We add CleverTap's app add-on, so your team can send app notifications from the same CleverTap dashboard.

If an app does not work properly

1. We test before launch. We test every app in the app, on iPhone and Android.

2. Backup option. If a page does not work properly, it can open in a browser window inside the app, where it works like the website.

Speed: what to expect

What to expect

The app loads your website, so pages load at the same speed as tucokids.com on a phone today. If a page is fast on the website, it is fast in the app. If it is slow on the website, it is slow in the app.

Some things help. The tab bar and opening screen are part of the app, so they respond instantly. Any speed improvement on the website also improves the app. With your approval, we can turn off trackers that are not needed in the app, such as Contentsquare or VWO, so pages load faster.

Process

01

Plan and set up accounts

Troopod

Finalise the tab bar, app name, icon and notifications. Help your team set up both developer accounts.

Tuco

Create Google Play and Apple developer accounts in Tuco's name (Apple needs a D-U-N-S number). Give us staff access to Checkout and Payments settings.

02

Build the app

Troopod

Build the shell around tucokids.com: tab bar, opening and offline screens, CleverTap notifications, smart links, UPI payments and app order tracking.

Tuco

Tell us which trackers we can turn off in the app.

03

Test

Troopod

Place real ₹1 orders with UPI, card and COD. Check login, rewards, reviews, the quiz and every app, and fix any issues.

Tuco

Test the app on your team's phones.

04

Submit to the app stores

Troopod

Prepare the store listings, privacy policy and data forms. Manage the Google and Apple review.

Tuco

Approve the final store listing.

05

Launch and track

Troopod

Add a "Get the app" banner to the website, send the launch notification, and share regular reports on installs, app orders and repeat orders.

Tuco

Share the launch on WhatsApp and CleverTap, add the QR code to pack inserts, and decide on an app-only offer.
