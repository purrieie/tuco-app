Limitations of a web app on iPhone
1
Not listed on the app stores

A search for "Tuco Kids" returns no result. There is no store listing and no ratings.

2
No install prompt on iPhone

Customers must install it manually through Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Most do not.

3
Restricted notifications

iPhone delivers notifications only after manual installation and permission.

4
Separate login

The home-screen version does not share the Safari login, so customers must log in again.

Recommendation: a wrapper app (the shell) delivers the same website within a store-listed app, which removes all four limitations.

Part 2 · Proposal

Tuco Kids Mobile App for Play Store and App Store

Troopod will build and publish a Tuco Kids app on the Play Store and App Store. The app loads tucokids.com as it is, including products, the quiz, reviews, rewards, videos and GoKwik checkout. The Shopify setup remains unchanged, and the Tuco team continues to manage the store as it does today. It is a full app on both stores, and building it around the existing website is the most efficient approach.

Scope

The app is an exact replica of tucokids.com, with the same pages, sections, products, prices, offers, apps, checkout and login. No part of the website is redesigned or rebuilt. Only the native shell around it is new: the bottom tab bar, notifications, opening and offline screens, and smart links.

Solution overview

A wrapper app loads the live website inside a native app. The shell is the native layer we build: the app icon, bottom navigation and notifications. Any update made to the website reflects in the app automatically.

Performance

Expected speed

The app loads the website, so page speed in the app matches tucokids.com on mobile today. Pages that are fast on the website are fast in the app, and pages that are slow on the website are slow in the app.

The tab bar and opening screen are native, so they respond instantly. Any speed improvement made to the website applies to the app as well. Subject to Tuco's approval, trackers not required in the app (such as Contentsquare or VWO) can be disabled to improve load times.

Prerequisite · store accounts in Tuco's name

The app is published under Tuco's own Apple and Google developer accounts, so ownership remains with Tuco. The Tuco team creates both accounts, pays the store fees directly, and adds our developer as a team member.

Apple Developer$99 a yearPayable to Apple. Requires a D-U-N-S number (a free company ID), which can take 1 to 2 weeks.
Google Play Console$25 one timePayable to Google. A company account is required, not a personal account.

If these accounts already exist, no new fee applies. Store fees are separate from our commercials. We will assist with the setup, which is scheduled first as it takes the longest.
